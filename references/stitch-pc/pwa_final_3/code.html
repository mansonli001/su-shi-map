history_edu Historical Context

In 1080, Su Shi was exiled to Huangzhou following the Crow Terrace Poetry Trial. Despite immense political pressure and poverty, this period marked his creative zenith. He built the "Snow Hall" (Xuetang) during a heavy winter storm, covering the walls with murals of snowscapes to reflect his pursuit of purity and resilience in adversity.
