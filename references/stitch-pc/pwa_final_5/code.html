timeline Life Trajectory

1037 - 1056

Meishan Youth

Born in Sichuan. Early years shaped by rigorous classical education and natural landscapes.

1057 - 1079

Early Career

Passed the imperial examination. Served in various posts across the empire before the Crow Terrace Poetry Trial.

1080 - 1084

Dongpo Layman

Exiled to Huangzhou. Embraced farming and adopted his famous pen name, producing his greatest literary works.

1094 - 1100

Southern Exile

Banished to Huizhou and later Danzhou (Hainan Island). Found peace in desolation.

1101

Return North

Pardoned, but passed away in Changzhou during his journey back.
